0.9.286
- Fixed: The automatic climb could hold a climb rate the aircraft could not sustain. On a heavy departure the speed schedule gave up after five minutes if the flaps were not yet up, then flew a fixed climb rate towards 250 knots for the rest of the climb
    - It no longer gives up on a clock, and it lets the speed rise past 250 while the last flaps are still coming up, as they need
    - If it does fall back to a plain climb rate, it aims for the right speed for the altitude and returns to speed hold once the aircraft is there, including when you hand the climb back to the crew
    - Climb rate changes are paced, come off quickly when speed is being lost, and are limited to 2,000 fpm above 10,000 ft and 1,500 above FL250. Every change is written to the log
- Added: The first officer calls N2 rising as each engine starts to turn
- Added: The takeoff calculator warns when the aircraft has not been loaded yet. With a flight plan loaded it compares the aircraft's weight with the plan's takeoff weight, under the Weight box and beside the speeds. Load with the aircraft's Fast Load or its load manager; either works
- Added: The crew say so when a Before Starting Engines item is waiting on you: gear lever, parking brake, start levers, a pulled fire handle, or low engine oil
- Changed: In their preflight the crew now set the switches the Before Starting Engines checklist asks about: pressurization mode to auto, outflow valves, equipment cooling and crew oxygen for the engineer; speed brake lever, flight control hydraulic power, autobrakes, stab trim cutout and flight directors for the first officer
- Added: The first officer says so when the flaps are not at the planned takeoff setting after the crew have set them, and it is listed under open items
- Changed: Anti-skid is switched on and its guard closed at lineup
- Changed: After takeoff the gear lever goes to off only once all the gear lights are out
- Changed: The After Starting Engines checklist cannot be called until all four engines are stable
- Changed: The Checklist page goes back to the top when a new checklist takes over
- Fixed: The Taxi checklist moved on from Flight controls before the control check was finished. It now waits for all three axes, rudder included, and each back to neutral
- Fixed: Set takeoff trim was greyed out before engine start with nothing to say why

0.9.221
First numbered release. Everything below is what the plugin does today.

- Added: A first officer and a flight engineer for the Felis 747-200 freighter. They run the flows and the callouts from the Felis handbooks, and only ever in that aircraft
- Added: Normal checklists, called and answered by the crew, with the items checked against the real switches and the current item highlighted as you go
    - Manual tap or automatic advance, your choice
    - Reference items read the aircraft and show you the value rather than asking you for it
    - Skip or reset a checklist at any point
- Added: QRH. The emergency and abnormal checklists, searchable, with the ones the crew can actually run for you marked
- Added: Takeoff performance calculator, built from the Performance Handbook tables for this aircraft
    - Assumed temperature derate, Rating I and Rating II, or no derate at all
    - V1, VR and V2, flap setting, thrust setting in EPR or N1
    - Field elevation, runway length, slope and wind filled in from X-Plane's own airport data and the live METAR
- Added: Engine start, APU and electrical power run by the engineer, on your word
- Added: Departure climb flown to a NADP profile, with the cleared altitude respected
- Added: In the cruise, the engineer watches fuel against the plan every hour, calls the step climb when the aircraft has burned down to it, and calls top of descent
- Added: The crew can ask SayIntentions ATC for the step climb and wait for the clearance before going up
- Added: The first officer sets the altitude window from SayIntentions ATC clearances, climb or descent, for the whole flight
- Added: Weather. METAR and TAF for both ends, and the ATIS from SayIntentions, FAA D-ATIS, VATSIM or IVAO
- Added: SimBrief flight plans, with the OFP on screen and the numbers filled in for you
- Added: Spoken crew callouts, with the volume and the accent your own
- Added: Cockpit lighting set for the time of day, anti-ice when the conditions call for it, and fuel managed through the flight
- Added: Pause the simulator approaching the destination, or when fuel reaches a figure you set
- Added: The panel. One page at a time down the left: flight, checklist, QRH, performance, weather, log and settings
    - Dark and light
    - Tuck it away to a small 747 on the edge of the screen, and click that to bring it back
    - Text size to taste
- Added: macOS and Linux versions
